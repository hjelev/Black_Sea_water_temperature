Frequently asked questions

What is the water temperature in Sochi, Russia today?

The sea water temperature in Sochi, Russia is 20.2°C (measured Oct 30, 2026). That is 1.1°C warmer than the 3-year average for this date (19.1°C).

When is the sea warmest in Sochi, Russia?

The warmest month is August, with an average water temperature of 27.8°C. The highest reading on record is 29.9°C (Jul 24, 2024).

When is the swimming season in Sochi, Russia?

The sea averages 20°C or more from June to October, which is the main swimming season. August is the warmest month at 27.8°C.

How cold does the sea get in Sochi, Russia?

The coldest month is February, averaging 10.3°C. The lowest reading on record is 9.4°C (Feb 21, 2023).

How long is the swimming season in Sochi, Russia?

The water typically stays at 20°C or above from June 5 to October 30 — about 145 days a year (median of 4 seasons).

What kind of beach is in Sochi, Russia?

The beaches in Sochi, Russia are mostly pebbly. Water shoes are handy, and the water is usually clearer than on sandy beaches because the waves stir up less sediment.

Why is the sea in Sochi, Russia sometimes suddenly cold?

Because of upwelling. When a steady wind blows along the coast or away from it for a day or two, it pushes the warm surface layer out to sea and cold water from below rises to replace it. The water can cool by 5–8°C within a day, even in midsummer, and usually warms up again a few days after the wind changes. The beach conditions panel above shows when upwelling is under way or likely.

Are there jellyfish in the sea at Sochi, Russia?

Sometimes. The most common is the moon jellyfish (Aurelia aurita), whose sting is very weak. In late summer the large barrel jellyfish (Rhizostoma pulmo) can appear; its sting causes mild, short-lived irritation. The transparent comb jellies don't sting at all. Numbers peak in warm water from July to September and vary a lot from year to year.

Are there tides in the Black Sea at Sochi, Russia?

Practically none. The Black Sea is almost enclosed, so the tidal range is only a few centimetres. Changes in the water level at Sochi, Russia come from wind and waves, not tides.
